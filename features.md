# Planned Features

## Core Functions

- ✅ Start screen with the title "Lucky 21 - BLACKJACK"
- ✅ Greeting and display of the current chip balance
- ✅ Bet input
- ✅ Display of the dealer's hidden card
- ✅ Display of the player's cards and current score
- ✅ Menu for "Draw Card", "Stand", and "End Game"

## Blackjack Rules

- ✅ Standard deck of 52 cards
- ✅ Values for number cards, face cards, and aces
- ✅ Blackjack detection
- ✅ Dealer draws until reaching at least 17 points
- ✅ Detection of wins, losses, and ties
- ✅ Payout and return of chips
- ✅ End of game at zero chips or at the player's request
